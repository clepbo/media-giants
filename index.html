FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Talk to us. We reply within 24 hours.

Do I need any experience?

No. The masterclass is 100% beginner friendly. We start from the fundamentals and guide you step by step until you can run profitable campaigns confidently.

Can I learn with only a phone?

Yes. A laptop gives you the full Ads Manager experience, but you can complete most lessons and even run campaigns from a smartphone. We show you exactly how.

Will I receive a certificate?

Yes. After completing the masterclass and its assignments, you receive an official Media Giants Digital Academy Certificate of Completion.

Will recordings be available?

Yes. Every live session is recorded and uploaded, and you keep lifetime access so you can revisit any lesson whenever you want.

Can business owners join?

Absolutely. Many students are business owners who want to run their own ads instead of relying on expensive agencies. Everything applies directly to your business.

How much should I budget for running ads?

You can practise with small daily budgets. We show you how to test cheaply before spending more, so you learn without wasting money.

How do I pay?

Pay securely by debit card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack or Flutterwave. Once payment is confirmed, you get access and an invite to the student group.
